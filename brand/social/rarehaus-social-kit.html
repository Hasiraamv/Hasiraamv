01

Avatar

The monogram is the avatar. The seal is not.

Instagram renders the avatar at 320 px on the profile and as small as 32 px beside a comment. Below roughly 64 px the arch closes up and the RH inside it fills in, so the profile picture uses the monogram alone. The full seal stays for platforms that give the avatar real estate — YouTube, LinkedIn, a browser tab is fine at 32 because it is geometry only.

02

Grid

Object, statement, record — then repeat.

The grid is read three across, so the rhythm is set by columns, not by single posts. One ivory statement per row keeps the profile from going flat black; two ivory posts in a row breaks it. Never place two product posts side by side — alternate the object with the record that proves it.

Row rhythm

Object → statement → drop. The record post (authentication or sold) closes a release and opens the next row.

Ivory ration

One ivory post per row, never two adjacent. It is the only light surface the brand has on feed.

Crop

Posts are built 4:5 and cropped to 1:1 on the grid. Keep the mark and the price inside the centre square — the templates already do.

Carousels

Object first, detail macro second, authentication record last. Never lead with the certificate.

03

Feed templates

Five posts. Everything else is a variation.

All built 1080×1350 — the tallest slot Instagram allows, and the one that takes the most of a scroll. Copy lives in brand/social/generate.py; change it there and re-run rather than editing the SVGs by hand.

Drop announcement

Release day and time only. No countdown stickers, no urgency copy.

Listing

One object, its grade, its ask, its serial. The frame is #191817, never pure black.

Statement

The only ivory post. Voice, not inventory — run at most one per row.

Record

The certificate as a post. Closes a release; never opens one.

Sold

Final price, stated flat. Proof the market cleared, not a victory lap.

04

05

Highlight covers

Icons only — the label is cropped away.

Instagram shows a highlight as a circle cut from the centre of a 1080×1920 image, so any word set on the cover disappears. The icon carries it; the highlight’s own title carries the word.

06

Captions

Two lines. The record does the selling.

Caption structure: what it is, then what is true about it. No hashtag walls — three at most, and only category tags a collector actually follows. Emoji never appear in brand-voice copy.

07

Cadence & specs

One release week, posted five times.

A release week is the unit. Outside a release week the account posts twice: one authentication record, one statement. Quiet is on-brand — a feed that posts daily is a shop, not an archive.
