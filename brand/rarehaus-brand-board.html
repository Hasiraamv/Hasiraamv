01

Positioning

A private archive that happens to be open to buyers.

RAREHAUS acquires, authenticates and resells rare sneakers, watches, bags and collectible fashion. Every piece is examined in-house, graded, serialised and released with a record of its condition. The identity is built to feel like a collection catalogue — restrained, exact, and quiet about how much it knows.

Exclusive

Scarcity is stated as fact, never shouted. Inventory counts do the work.

Verified

Every claim is backed by a serial, a grade and a named authenticator.

Cultured

Fluent in the objects. Release years, colourways, references, movements.

Selective

What we decline defines us more than what we list.

08

Authentication & quality control

Five checks, in this order, every time.

The sequence is fixed because the identity claims it is. Where authentication imagery appears, shoot the process — gloved hands, callipers, loupe, UV — never a stock “certified” badge.

01Intake & serialisationPiece is weighed, measured and assigned its permanent RH serial before handling.
02ConstructionStitch density, glue lines, lasting, hardware weight against reference samples held on site.
03MaterialsLeather grain, suede nap, mesh weave and lining under 10× loupe and UV.
04Marks & labelsSize tags, date codes, movement references and font weights compared digit by digit.
05Grade & recordDS, VNDS, EXC or GOOD assigned, photographed, signed by a named authenticator.
—DeclinedAnything unresolved is returned. No conditional passes, no “likely authentic”.

09

Art direction

Light it like a vitrine, not a shop floor.

One directional key, hard-edged, raked across the object at roughly 45°; everything else falls away. Shadows stay long and soft-edged. Colour grading holds low saturation with a warm bias so ivory never goes blue.

Surfaces

Honed basalt, brushed steel, smoked glass, raw ivory paper. Nothing glossy, nothing marbled.

Lighting

Single 90 cm gridded softbox at 45°, 1/4 CTO, black flag opposite. No fill card on hero shots.

Composition

One object, one plinth, generous negative space. Overhead flat-lay or three-quarter — never both in one release.

Detail

Macro on stitch, nap, mesh, bezel knurling. The texture is the proof.

People

Hands only, gloved, mid-task. The brand is the archive, not a model.

Never

Confetti, gradients, neon rim light, floating products, crowds, graffiti walls.

10

Voice

State the fact. Let scarcity speak.

Short declaratives. Numbers instead of adjectives. The brand never uses urgency language it cannot substantiate with inventory.

We say

—12 lots. Opens 18:00 CET.
—Examined in-house against reference samples.
—Graded DS. Serialised RH-SNK-04127-A.
—We declined four pieces this week.

We never say

×Hurry — almost gone!
×100% legit, guaranteed authentic.
×The ultimate luxury flex.
×Grails you can’t miss 🔥
